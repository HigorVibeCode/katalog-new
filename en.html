Technical data

Glass thickness
8 to 12 mm

Made to measure, no minimum size. Up to 3000 × 2000 mm per pane.

Your choice: rectangle, round arch, segmental arch, pointed arch, quarter arch, slanted, rounded, chamfer, wave, your own design or a template. Curved glass too, for example with a 900 mm (C) or 260 mm (J) radius.

Any texture, any relief. Float glass sandblasted too.
Color
Clear or colored, any color.

With or without LED in the edge, any light color.

02 Sink

Technical data

Glass thickness
19 mm
Dimensions
Depending on the model, L 65 to 210 cm, D 35 to 70 cm. Corner sinks up to about 95 × 95 cm.
Shape
Any outline, also as a corner sink. Basin shape set by the model, others on request. Glass back panel if you like.
Construction
Basin and top in one piece, or a glass top with a glass vessel bowl.
Basin
Curved down to the drain, no flat bottom, at most 15 cm deep.
Surface
Smooth on top. Underside Float (smooth), Minimal or Texture.
Color
On the underside, solid or as a gradient, any color – or clear, without color.
Light
With or without LED in the edge at the wall, any light color.

03 Kitchen · Backsplash

Technical data

Glass thickness
6 to 8 mm
Dimensions
Made to measure, no minimum size. Up to 3000 × 2000 mm per pane, longer walls in several panes.
Shape
Rectangular or free-form: curved top edge, arch, a sweep around the range hood. Around corners too. Cutouts and holes for outlets, switches and fixtures.
Surface
Smooth at the front. At the back Float (smooth), any texture, any relief.
Color
On the back, solid or as a gradient, any color. The relief in an accent color if you like.
Light
No LED in the glass. Lights under the wall cabinets or a shelf make texture and relief glow, any light color.
